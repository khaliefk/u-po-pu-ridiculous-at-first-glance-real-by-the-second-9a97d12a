# Ridiculous at First Glance, Real by the Second

One sentence, 200 characters at most, pitching a bizarre startup idea that
sounds absurd until you realise it would actually sell. A judge model picks
the pitch that is both strangest and soundest.

Bounty: https://make.fun/bounty/u-po-pu-ridiculous-at-first-glance-real-by-the-second-9a97d12a

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

It writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt`.

## The entry

> We sell monthly excuse insurance: cancel any plan and a former diplomat sends your friends an excuse so gracious they thank you for it, so chronic flakers pay to stay liked.

173 characters, one sentence. The bizarre bit is excuse insurance itself;
the business holds because chronic flakers will pay a professional wordsmith
to keep their friendships intact.
